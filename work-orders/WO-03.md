# WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.

## Summary

**WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.** Plus two fixture strategies in `tests/fixtures/`:
`rigged.py` (uses `close.shift(-1)` and flips position every bar) and
`clean.py` (volatility-scaled trend on SPY/QQQ/TLT/GLD, daily tier).

Status: **built 2026-11-13 PT; one acceptance line needs the owner's call.**

Deviation: the daily tier holds S&P 500 constituents only, so SPY/QQQ/TLT/GLD
are not in it. The real-data run below uses daily bars aggregated from the
intraday tier (`factory.data.daily_splits_from_intraday`): 452 IS sessions,
2023-11-13 to 2025-09-03. The session the intraday split cuts through is
dropped. No threshold or fixture parameter was changed.

Recorded results on that data (IS, thresholds from `factory/evals/config.py`):

| Fixture | Eval 1 | Sharpe at 0 / 2 / 5 / 10 bps | Eval 2 | Break-even bps |
|---------|--------|------------------------------|--------|----------------|
| `rigged.py` | FAIL: SPY at 2024-05-28T04:00:00+00:00 (30% cut) | not run | not run | n/a |
| `rigged_fixed.py` (leak removed) | pass | 1.28 / 0.97 / 0.505 / -0.27 | pass, by 0.005 | 5.03 |
| `clean.py` | pass | 0.84 / 0.82 / 0.78 / 0.73 | pass | above 10 |

Against the acceptance criteria:

- rigged fails Eval 1 naming a timestamp and symbol: **met** (real and synthetic data).
- clean passes Eval 1: **met**. On Eval 2 it passes with its first-written
  parameters (lookback 126, vol window 20); it was not tuned.
- rigged with the leak removed fails Eval 2: **met on synthetic bars
  (unit test), not met on the real sample.** Flipping SPY long/short every day
  happened to earn a gross Sharpe of 1.28 over these 452 days, leaving 0.505 at
  5 bps against a 0.5 threshold. (An earlier run that wrongly kept a 6-minute
  partial session scored 0.494.) It then fails Eval 3 (worst jitter 17% of
  base), Eval 4 (OOS Sharpe -2.3) and Eval 5 (DSR ~0). The threshold and the
  fixture were left alone; a 452-day sample is simply too short to separate
  this from luck at Eval 2. A longer sample (ETFs added to the daily tier,
  2016 onward) would be the honest way to tighten this.

## Acceptance Criteria

rigged fails Eval 1 naming a timestamp and symbol; with the leak
removed it fails Eval 2; clean passes Eval 1. Record what clean does on Eval 2
honestly; do not tune it until it passes.
