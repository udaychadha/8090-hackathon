# WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.

## Summary

**WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.** Plus two fixture strategies in `tests/fixtures/`:
`rigged.py` (uses `close.shift(-1)` and flips position every bar) and
`clean.py` (volatility-scaled trend on SPY/QQQ/TLT/GLD, daily tier).

Status: **accepted, 2026-11-13 PT**, with one deviation the owner should confirm.

Deviation: the daily tier holds S&P 500 constituents only, so SPY/QQQ/TLT/GLD
are not in it. The real-data run below uses daily bars aggregated from the
intraday tier's IS split (`factory.data.daily_from_intraday`): 453 sessions,
2023-11-13 to 2025-09-04. No threshold or fixture parameter was changed.

Recorded results on that data (IS, thresholds from `factory/evals/config.py`):

| Fixture | Eval 1 | Sharpe at 0 / 2 / 5 / 10 bps | Eval 2 | Break-even bps |
|---------|--------|------------------------------|--------|----------------|
| `rigged.py` | FAIL: SPY at 2024-05-28T04:00:00+00:00 (30% cut) | not run | not run | n/a |
| `rigged_fixed.py` (leak removed) | pass | 1.27 / 0.96 / 0.49 / -0.28 | FAIL | 4.96 |
| `clean.py` | pass | 0.83 / 0.81 / 0.78 / 0.73 | pass | above 10 |

`rigged_fixed.py` fails Eval 2 by a narrow margin (0.494 against 0.5): flipping
SPY long/short every day happened to earn a gross Sharpe of 1.27 over these 453
days, and only the 2 turnover per day at 5 bps pulls it under. `clean.py`
passes Eval 2 with its first-written parameters (lookback 126, vol window 20);
it was not tuned.

## Acceptance Criteria

removed it fails Eval 2; clean passes Eval 1. Record what clean does on Eval 2
honestly; do not tune it until it passes.
