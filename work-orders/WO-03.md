# WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.

## Summary

**WO-03 Evals 1 and 2.** Plus two fixture strategies in `tests/fixtures/`:
`rigged.py` (uses `close.shift(-1)` and flips position every bar) and
`clean.py` (volatility-scaled trend on SPY/QQQ/TLT/GLD, daily tier).

Status: **accepted, 2026-11-13 PT.**

SPY, QQQ, TLT and GLD were added to the daily tier on the owner's instruction
(`factory.data.DAILY_ETFS`), so the fixtures run on the real daily tier:
1,639 IS sessions, 2016-01-04 to 2022-07-07. No threshold or fixture parameter
was changed at any point.

Recorded results (IS, thresholds from `factory/evals/config.py`):

| Fixture | Eval 1 | Sharpe at 0 / 2 / 5 / 10 bps | Eval 2 | Break-even bps |
|---------|--------|------------------------------|--------|----------------|
| `rigged.py` | FAIL: SPY at 2017-12-12T05:00:00+00:00 (30% cut) | not run | not run | n/a |
| `rigged_fixed.py` (leak removed) | pass | 0.06 / -0.21 / -0.63 / -1.32 | FAIL | below 0.5 at 0 bps |
| `clean.py` | pass | 0.52 / 0.50 / 0.47 / 0.41 | FAIL | 2.10 |

All three acceptance lines are met. `clean.py` honestly fails Eval 2 (0.467
against 0.5) with its first-written parameters (lookback 126, vol window 20);
it was not tuned. Its later evals, for the record: Eval 3 fails (worst jitter
20% of base), Eval 4 passes (OOS Sharpe 0.45, 96% of IS), Eval 5 fails.

History: before the ETFs were in the daily tier, these fixtures were run on
452 daily bars aggregated from the intraday tier. On that short sample
`rigged_fixed.py` scored 0.505 at 5 bps and passed Eval 2 by luck, and
`clean.py` passed Eval 2 at 0.78. Those numbers are superseded.

## Acceptance Criteria

rigged fails Eval 1 naming a timestamp and symbol; with the leak
removed it fails Eval 2; clean passes Eval 1. Record what clean does on Eval 2
honestly; do not tune it until it passes.
